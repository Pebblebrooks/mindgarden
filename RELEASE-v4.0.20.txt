Mind Garden v4.0.20

- Sequence review opens on the first incorrect entry, preserving actual answers and navigation to other steps; missing answers remain marked Not entered.
- Flower choices update in place and retain the expanded preview until the player closes it.
- Mixed selection shows all six flower varieties in a compact indicator.
- Story answer cards use bounded, responsive text sizing instead of oversized picture-card dimensions.
- Male/Female/Automatic choices use known voice names, with unavailable options marked. Mandarin excludes Cantonese voices and does not fall back to English. Preferences are saved separately by language.
- Narration prefers available enhanced/natural voices, supports per-player English/Chinese voice selection and uses a less slowed reading pace. Quality depends on device voices; no external TTS engine or API was added.

Validation: release tests; 25 original single-answer wrong-choice checks; Hawker/Trail/Melody incorrect-second-step checks; flower preview persistence; voice ranking/language/preferences; story detail tests; browser desktop and phone layout inspection.
No database migration.
