MIND GARDEN v3.8.6 — CHANGES SINCE v3.8.5

WELCOME, CONTROLS AND ACCESSIBILITY
- Centered button labels and music actions; music play uses a solid triangle.
- Bouquet pause uses ||. Standard rotation takes about 24 seconds per turn;
  Gentle takes about 36 seconds. Pause and motion-off remain available.
- Navbar T-/T+ shows percentage presets from 80% to 200%, plus reset to 100%.
  Card labels and action text scale with the preference, and layouts wrap.
- Main navigation centered; utility controls right-aligned with responsive rows.
- Fixed the welcome screen remaining visible above a newly started game.
- Fixed multiplayer name editor closing after Save name.

PERSONAL DAILY PRACTICE
- Today's Games selects the three lowest average results across each game's
  last five valid local results for the current player. Fewer than three played
  activities are supplemented with other activities and an explanatory message.
- Players can choose a comfortable practice level before each activity.
- Matching Pairs and the five newer games now participate in daily completion.
- Keeping a game after a leave confirmation preserves the daily journey.
- Results can retain level information through JSON export/import.
- The selection uses recorded game results (accuracy/completion/wins depending
  on the activity), not a clinical assessment or a comparable skill test.

DIFFICULTY
- Reworked progression across the 30 original mini-games. Higher levels use
  longer memory loads, more distractors, larger numbers and more complex rules.
- Advanced Number Patterns includes growing differences, interleaved sequences,
  weighted recurrences and mixed operations. Numeric displays resist automatic
  translation. Spelled-out number answers were not an intended feature; the
  reported word display was not reproduced in the source or local review.
- Stronger mental arithmetic, calendar/time and market-change challenges.
- Complete the Saying expands answer choices; harder dominoes uses stronger
  CPU move selection. Existing board-size progression in pairs, puzzles,
  pathways and bingo is retained.

MULTIPLAYER
- Eight additional online activities: Times tables, Missing number, Market
  change, Calendar day, Shape patterns, Find the picture, Match the word and
  Same group. The host has 17 choices across 13 activity types.
- Completed room rankings remain available after closure/expiry; participants
  can revisit total and round scores. Equal totals share a rank.
- Existing mg_results remains the score store. New mg_rounds stores activity
  metadata. Missing metadata on older rounds is explicitly labelled.
- New quiz answers are checked by the server. Participant-only history access,
  cancellation, withdrawal and one-active-room restrictions are retained.
- Online history follows this browser's online identity. Local backup imports
  do not restore online identity credentials.
