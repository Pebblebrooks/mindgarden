KNOWN LIMITATIONS — NOT CLAIMED AS COMPLETE
- Wildlife remains stylized 3D/GIF, not photorealistic filmed animals.
  This browser had no WebGL, so GIF fallback was verified; 3D playback needs
  a WebGL-capable device check. Fallback has fewer species.
- Nature calls/waves are synthesized. Instrument arrangements use recorded
  samples. A complete live orchestra recording is not included.
- Profiles, photos, video and preferences live in this browser/device;
  there are no cross-device personal accounts/cloud backups.
- Personal music files must be selected again after reloading; the saved
  preferences are not a persistent uploaded music library.
- Room broadcasts reach active Play Together lobby sessions, not every user
  merely viewing the homepage and not people outside the app.
- Live Cloudflare room delivery between physical devices has not been checked
  in this repair. Automated backend/DOM-client results are not that test.
- Mobile layout has responsive rules; this run checked desktop and larger
  text. It did not repeat a complete phone/tablet hardware matrix.
- Broad Chinese coverage exists; this repair checked relevant settings and
  multiplayer flows, not every sentence in every possible game state.
- Digital pet feeding/stroking, Simple Home, remote caregiver portal,
  commercialization/pricing and synchronized accounts were recommendations,
  not approved implementations, and have not been added.
