# Mind Garden 5.0.10

- Desktop solo games use a centred 820px surface with a shared 720px content column. Story reading, answer rows and footer controls have deliberate spacing; long activities scroll separately from controls. Story answers no longer receive picture-grid sizing.
- Restored the three original gardens and ten later architectural scenes alongside the nine existing choices: 22 paired day/dusk scenes. Architectural revisions remove creature roof ornaments and decorative symbolic medallions. Neutral names replace sacred/fantasy labels. Saved valid scene IDs remain selectable.
- Background-only CSS reduces saturation/contrast and warms dusk rendering. Personal photos and videos retain their original appearance.
- Five birds (including three smaller, distant birds) and four butterflies in Standard; Gentle shows one nearer bird, two distant birds and two butterflies. Bird routes use reviewed source-image sky rectangles projected through the actual centred cover crop. If the visible crop has no safe sky, birds are hidden. Personal media has no authored sky region, so birds are hidden there.
- About reviewed and updated in both languages; retired-scene claims removed. Setup/menu paths remain unchanged.

## Artwork

Built-in image generation edited ten supplied historical scene references. Each output contains a matching day/dusk pair, split into WebP assets under `public/garden-<scene>-day.webp` and `public/garden-<scene>-night.webp`. Prompt: preserve distinctive viewpoint, buildings and landscape; replace dragons, creature ornaments, sacred symbols and medallions with plain stone and unadorned roof ends; secular traditional garden architecture; restrained natural daylight and warm neutral dusk; no blue/purple wash, HDR glow, labels or people. Existing original gardens are restored from repository history and toned only at render time.
