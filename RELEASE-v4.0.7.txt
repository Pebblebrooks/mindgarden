Mind Garden V4.0.7 — approved release, 8 November 2026

NEW: Everyday Life category; Today & Here (date/day/month/year/time/selected
country, typed or choice answers), Food & Kitchen (food groups, ingredients,
utensils), Around Us (dated Singapore source-based recall pilot).
All three support English/Chinese, three levels, hints, skip, replay and next
level. Hinted answers do not earn independent-answer credit.

FIXES: host room-closure responses lacking player lists no longer crash guest
rankings rendering; hidden Next button is truly hidden until an answer; mobile
activity spacing leaves clearance for the floating sound control.

PETS: panel above background wildlife; centered titles; Cosy Corner category;
predecoded real recordings, shared playback/status, gentle audio fades and
species/action-matched sounds. Audio starts only after user interaction.

LIMITS: no claim of a medical assessment or treatment. Country is user-selected,
not detected. Date/time depends on the device clock. Around Us is a single
editorial pilot with expiry, not a live feed. New activities are single-player.
Pet visuals are frame-based, not fully rigged 3D. Physical-device audio and live
Cloudflare multiplayer require post-deployment acceptance testing.
