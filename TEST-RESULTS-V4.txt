LIMITS
No production deployment or live Cloudflare D1 change/test was performed. Multiplayer checks used test fixtures/local SQLite-backed requests. Browser validation used desktop Chromium with simulated widths, not physical iOS/Safari or Android devices. This was not a new WebGL performance audit. Randomised games mean samples are not every possible picture/question. No recruited older-adult usability session or formal WCAG/PDPA certification is claimed. The included usability checklist describes the remaining real-participant work.
