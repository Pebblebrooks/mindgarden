Implemented
- My Games questionnaire: selected games, starting level, short/standard/long question sessions, time preference, 5/10/15 progression steps, Gentle/Balanced/Challenging ranges and per-game overrides.
- My Games welcome card placed in the card grid; direct browser click verified after resolving welcome-scene overlap.
- Individual profile persistence, backup/import support, input validation, and stable underlying challenge metadata. Existing scores are retained.
- Individual game information buttons: descriptions and skills practised; mouse hover, keyboard focus, tap, Escape and outside-click support.
- Optional unscored practice, next/easier level choices, repeat-success/difficulty suggestions, and navigation through the selected games.
- More varied missing-number positions, story settings, reverse sequence recall, picture deck sampling, related-object matching, graded puzzle/recall sizes, subtle spot-the-difference changes, and optional delayed recall.
- Timed matching retains its existing countdown; other personal games use an optional three-minute time goal without forced failure. Garden Pets remains unscored.
- Centered text, responsive preference cards, accessible large controls, contained decorative overflow, and immediate navbar fitting after resize/rebuild.
- About/user-guide progression description updated. App build/cache identifiers set to 4.0.24.

Design details
- Five or ten visible steps sample the selected challenge range. Underlying difficulty is saved separately from the visible step number.
- Some progression steps share a board size or difficulty family and generate varied content. They are not fifteen different board sizes.
- Personal settings apply to solo/daily play. Online room rules and host-selected difficulty remain shared across participants.
- Question session lengths apply to question games; a board game ends when its board is completed. Commercial previews retain their existing game-specific session structure.
