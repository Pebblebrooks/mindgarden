MIND GARDEN V4 — OLDER-ADULT USABILITY SESSION
This is a proposed test plan, not a record of completed participant testing.

Invite a small, varied initial group, for example 5–8 older adults, including people with low vision, different technology experience, and both English and Chinese readers. Use their usual phones/tablets where possible. Ask permission before observing or recording. Use test nicknames and test rooms; avoid collecting personal health information.

Ask each person to do these tasks without coaching first:
1. Start today's first activity and finish or stop it comfortably.
2. Make text larger, then restore 100%.
3. Find an activity in All Games and choose an easier/harder level.
4. Identify the pictures in matching pairs, bingo and picture clues.
5. Select Quiet view, then restore Garden view without losing progress.
6. Find the hamburger menu, change language or appearance, and return home.
7. Turn welcome animation/sound off, then on.
8. Use the scenery arrows and identify the selected scenery.
9. Join a prepared multiplayer room, complete a round, leave, and find the saved ranking.
10. Export a test-player backup and restore it as a separate profile.
11. Find help in About and open Contact without submitting private information.

Record for each task: completed independently, needed help, wrong turns, missed controls, accidental taps, unreadable text/pictures, discomfort, and the participant's own description. Observe whether they understand Quiet/Garden and the distinction between local progress and online rankings.

Include portrait/landscape rotation, 140% and 200% text, light/dark/high-contrast modes, reduced motion, touch input and an interrupted connection. Physical Safari/iPhone and Android checks remain necessary; a desktop browser simulation is not a substitute.

Prioritize issues that prevent a task or cause data loss/confusion, then improve readability and discoverability. Retest changed tasks with participants. Do not infer medical benefit or accessibility certification from game scores or this checklist.
