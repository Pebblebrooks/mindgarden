Approved v4.0.17 release audit. Base: v4.0.16.

## Verified coverage

- 1,800 complete original-game sessions: 30 games × 15 levels × English/Chinese × light/dark state; 10,920 rounds. DOM simulation checks answer availability, correct scoring, retained results, explicit progression, level-specific records. The default all-level-sessions.test.cjs runner shards into 15 processes; individual batches use MG_AUDIT_LEVEL=1 through 15. An initial unsharded experiment exceeded the test runner memory budget.
- 72 complete sessions across six matching-pairs sizes and ten board/garden activities at all three levels, both languages.
- 36 complete commercial-preview sessions: six games × three levels × two languages, including level-specific saved results and shared-team labelling.
- Everyday-game completion, clock/date/year boundaries, local-news validity, typed input and hints; existing test suite passed.
- Multiplayer readiness and expanded-activity UI/API suites passed, including saved results and room history.
- Browser layout checks: 2,700 original-game opening screens at 390, 768 and 1366 pixel widths, all 15 levels, light/dark; no horizontal overflow in checked controls/headings.
- Browser layout checks: 1,368 result/custom screens at those widths, 100% and 200% text, both themes. Original-game boundary levels 1/15 plus 18 custom games at all three levels; no horizontal overflow in checked controls/headings. Vertical scrolling is intentional.
- Rating tests cover timed thresholds, untimed completion, mismatches/hints, and finite sound/photo/team pools.

## Changes

- Separate score chart and table by selected player, game and difficulty. Legacy records without difficulty remain a separate group. Uses existing retained history (up to 300 games), latest 20 plotted results.
- Preview completions save practice results by level; Team Garden explicitly labels shared team results. No growth points awarded by previews. Garden Pets remains unscored.
- Synchronous original questions avoid the most recent 40 question signatures per player/game/level/language while the page remains open, with bounded retries for finite pools.
- Sound and photo sessions sample without replacement; team rounds have distinct counts. Arithmetic repetition tested over 25 fresh questions. Photo difficulty now varies the number of choices and session length with the available photos.
- Hawker/Trail/Melody signal the first incorrect sequence input and display unentered positions. Everyday Skip retains answer review. Bingo, sayings, spot-the-difference, dot mistakes and typed recall have sound cues respecting the game-sound setting. Spot selections and recall submissions ignore paused input.
- Matching pairs reports attempts, mismatches, hints and time. Timed stars remain speed-only: under 50% of allowance = 3, under 80% = 2, otherwise successful completion = 1. Untimed result uses a completion message instead of stars. Existing historical stars are preserved.
- Online rankings show accessible gold/silver/bronze trophy icons with numeric ranks; ties retain the same rank.

- Optional score and matching-award explanations now use accessible information icons: hover/focus/tap, Escape/outside dismissal. Main instructions and result feedback stay visible.

## Limits / remaining manual verification

- This is automated functional and browser-layout verification, not proof of every possible random board or every device/browser combination.
- Real iPad/iPhone Safari touch, voice availability, audio audibility and interruption recovery still require physical-device review. Chromium iframe widths are viewport simulations, not those operating systems.
- Browser layout matrix checks bounds, not exhaustive semantic instruction quality, colour-contrast certification or every result state at every level.
- Date/time facts, finite learning sets, repeated symbols within valid memory sequences, and intentional Replay can recur. Never-repeat forever is not possible; no fabricated answers are introduced.
- Existing scores with no recorded level cannot safely be assigned a level. Historical progress remains unchanged.
- viewport-audit.html is an internal QA fixture. Copy to a local-only served path for browser checks; exclude it and public/qa-review.html from production packaging.

## Fast matching input follow-up

Single-player discarded all taps during its 1.1-second mismatch lock. A new tap now closes the wrong pair and starts the next turn immediately, including a tap on either previous wrong card. Delayed callbacks are isolated from subsequent turns and new games. Multiplayer now also accepts a previous mismatched card as a new first card. Study, pause and already-matched cards retain their input guards. Six single-player sizes and both online matching sizes are covered by fast-tap regression tests. Physical-device verification remains outstanding.

## Shared immediate press feedback

All game and multiplayer-modal buttons now show a neutral press outline on contact, before native click activation. The feedback layer never submits answers, prevents default scrolling, or synthesizes duplicate clicks. Pointer cancellation/movement clears the outline; disabled controls do not acknowledge input. Keyboard press/release is supported. Melody note buttons visibly disable during pattern playback and re-enable when it is the player's turn. Intentional study, pause and answer-review guards remain.
